# Cialdini's Pre-Suasion: Visual Primes & Privileged Moments

Extracted and adapted from Robert B. Cialdini's work *Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade*.

## 1. The Core Concept: The Privileged Moment

Pre-suasion is the practice of getting people ready to say yes to a message before they experience it. It is not what you say in the message, but what you introduce into the environment *immediately before* the message is delivered.

- **The Privileged Moment**: A brief window in time directly preceding a proposition where the prospect's mindset is uniquely malleable.
- **The Golden Rule**: 'What is focal is presumed causal'. Whatever draws the viewer's attention first is subconsciously assumed to be the most important factor in the entire equation.

## 2. Attentional Channeling & Single-Chute Framing

### Attentional Channeling
You can steer human decision-making simply by directing where attention lands first:
- If you ask someone if they are unhappy with their life, they will search their memory exclusively for unhappy memories and report higher unhappiness.
- **Single-Chute Questions**: Directing attention down a one-way cognitive chute where only one answer can be easily generated.
  - Example: 'Do you suddenly get the urge to run to the bathroom when you enter Target?'
  - The brain does not search for times they did not get the urge. It exclusively searches for the times they *did*, confirming the premise immediately.

## 3. The 0-3 Second Visual Prime in Short-Form Video

In short-form video, pre-suasion occurs before the user has even processed the first spoken word. The first 30 frames (0.0s to 1.0s) prime the entire cognitive frame:

| Visual Prime Dimension | Implementation Example | Subconscious Attentional Priming |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Authority Prime** | Creator sitting in car wearing surgical scrubs and stethoscope | Signals high medical authority, clinical urgency, emergency insider knowledge |
| **The Disgust / Visceral Prime** | Holding up a murky jar of black fluid with floating debris | Triggers primal survival reflex (LF8-1), forces immediate visual fixation |
| **The Deception / Audit Prime** | Standing in a supermarket aisle holding a bright brand-name cereal box | Primes suspicion, consumer betrayal, hidden danger in everyday spaces |
| **The Domestic Sanctuary Prime** | Warm wooden cutting board, organic lemons, glass mason jars, rustic lighting | Primes safety, wholesome purity, anti-synthetic natural healing |
| **The Anatomical Prime** | Holding a silicone spine model or 3D gut cross-section to camera | Primes biomechanical expertise, tangible root-cause diagnosis |

## 4. Geographies of Influence & Spatial Psychology

The physical environment where a video is filmed acts as an omnipresent pre-suasive anchor:

1. **The Driver's Seat (The Automotive Sanctuary)**:
   - High intimacy, zero corporate varnish, unfiltered authenticity.
   - The creator looks like a busy professional sharing a spontaneous, unscripted revelation on their commute.
2. **The Commercial Grocery Aisle**:
   - The frontline of consumer defense.
   - Placing the camera behind shelf products creates a sense of investigative espionage.
3. **The Pristine Herbal Kitchen**:
   - Natural light, mortar and pestle, dried herbs.
   - Primes traditional wisdom, slow medicine, and unhurried craftsmanship.
4. **The White Clinical Office**:
   - Diplomas on the wall, neutral tones, computer monitor showing scans.
   - Primes institutional authority and formal scientific rigor.

## 5. Sensory & Acoustic Priming

- **Sonic Cueing**: A sudden low-frequency thud, vinyl record crackle, or ambient supermarket chatter primes either high alarm or comforting realism.
- **Lighting Temperature**: Cool, clinical 5600K lighting primes surgical precision; warm 3200K amber lighting primes soothing recovery and organic safety.
