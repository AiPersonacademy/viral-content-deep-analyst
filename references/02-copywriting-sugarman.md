# Sugarman's Adweek Copywriting: Slippery Slide & Triggers

Extracted and adapted from Joseph Sugarman's direct-response masterwork *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*.

## 1. The Core Concept: The Slippery Slide

Copywriting is an engineered slide where every element compels the audience down to the next without friction:

- **The Sole Purpose of the First Element**: The only purpose of the headline (visual/spoken hook) is to get you to read or watch the first sentence.
- **The Sole Purpose of the First Sentence**: To get you to watch the second sentence.
- **The Sole Purpose of the Second Sentence**: To get you to watch the third sentence, and so on.
- **Momentum Engineering**: By keeping sentences short, easy to understand, and packed with intrigue, you build momentum. Once the viewer starts down the slide, it is physically uncomfortable for them to stop.

## 2. The 15 Axioms of Copywriting

1. Copywriting is a mental process the successful execution of which reflects the sum total of all your life experiences, knowledge, and ability to process information.
2. All elements in an advertisement are primarily designed to do only one thing: get you to read the first sentence of the copy.
3. The sole purpose of the first sentence is to get you to read the second sentence.
4. To create a slippery slide, you must create a compelling environment that draws the prospect into your message.
5. Get the prospect to say 'yes' and agree with you throughout the message.
6. The reader should be unable to resist reading your message, as if sliding down an oiled slide.
7. Always anticipate the prospect's objections and answer them before they raise them.
8. Every piece of copy should be focused on one single primary concept.
9. Curiosity is one of the most powerful motivators in the human psyche.
10. You sell on emotion, but you justify a purchase with logic.
11. Ideas are the most valuable assets in direct marketing.
12. The more familiar a product or remedy feels, the more comfortable the consumer is in accepting it.
13. Always make your copy specific. Specific facts build instant credibility.
14. The editing process is where great copy is created. Eliminate every word that causes hesitation.
15. Never sell a product. Sell a concept, an identity, and a transformation.

## 3. The 23 Elements of Short-Form Video Copy

Adapted from Sugarman's 23 print elements into video anatomy:

1. **Visual Hook**: Opening frame prop, motion, or facial expression (t = 0.0s).
2. **Text Hook Banner**: High-contrast, easily scannable text on the top or center of the frame.
3. **Spoken First Sentence**: Under 10 words, rapid cadence, provocative premise.
4. **Curiosity Seed**: Micro-loop ('and what happened next shocked the entire clinic').
5. **Pattern Interrupt**: Rapid shift in camera angle, audio track, or visual asset.
6. **Problem Statement**: Articulating the acute symptom with clinical specificity.
7. **Empathetic Mirroring**: 'I know you've tried everything and nothing worked'.
8. **Villain Identification**: Pinpointing the cause (seed oils, synthetic fillers, bad advice).
9. **Hero Mechanism**: Revealing the novel concept or ancient remedy.
10. **Scientific / Traditional Credibility**: Citing studies, historical lineages, or doctor backing.
11. **Visual Demonstration**: Physical proof (mixing, slicing, filtering, testing).
12. **Simplicity Reassurance**: 'You only need 2 minutes before bed'.
13. **Objection Preemption**: 'No, this won't upset your stomach'.
14. **Specific Measurement**: '1/4 teaspoon, not a drop more'.
15. **Emotional Relief**: Describing the feeling of waking up unburdened.
16. **Logical Justification**: Explaining the biochemical or physiological reason why it works.
17. **Social Proof Snippet**: Mentioning thousands who experienced the same breakthrough.
18. **Contrast Framing**: Showing the expensive pharmaceutical route vs the simple pantry solution.
19. **Urgency Hook**: Why addressing this today prevents severe chronic deterioration.
20. **Call to Action (CTA)**: Single clear directive (e.g. 'Comment TEA below').
21. **Incentive for Action**: 'I will send you the full measurement chart to your DMs'.
22. **Loop Anchor**: Closing sentence that seamlessly transitions back into the first sentence for infinite replay loops.
23. **Soundtrack Harmony**: Audio mood that amplifies urgency or calm relief.

## 4. Sugarman's 31 Psychological Triggers

Sugarman's classic triggers categorized for video engagement:

- **Belonging & Identity**: Feeling part of an enlightened, health-conscious tribe.
- **Honesty & Raw Vulnerability**: Admitting a flaw or past failure makes all other claims believable.
- **Credibility & Authority**: Unshakable confidence, professional poise, physical emblems.
- **Proof of Value**: Showing why the remedy is worth 100x the effort required to make it.
- **Desire to Belong**: Humans hate being on the outside of an inside secret.
- **Curiosity**: The irresistible urge to close an open informational gap.
- **Guilt & Self-Neglect**: Gently reminding viewers how they have ignored their own vitality.
- **Simplicity**: Offering a clear, friction-free path forward.
- **Specifics**: '347 patients' is vastly more persuasive than 'hundreds of patients'.
