# Forensic Multimodal Sensory Labs: Complete Inspection Protocols

The operational testing protocol for multimodal inspection of video, audio, and language.

## Lab 1: Acoustic & Prosody Lab

Examines the spoken auditory track and sound design:

1. **Delivery Cadence & Speed**:
   - Words Per Minute (WPM): Measure total words spoken in 0-3s hook vs body. (Target viral hook: 165-210 WPM).
   - Pauses & Micro-Breaks: Are there gaps longer than 0.2s? Top hooks eliminate all dead air.
2. **Pitch Modulation & Inflection**:
   - Downward Inflection: Commands certainty and authority at the end of statements.
   - Upward Inflection: Creates curiosity and question loops.
3. **Audio Layering**:
   - Primary vocal track (A-roll).
   - Sound effects (whooshes, thuds, riser strings).
   - Background music track (mood, tempo, BPM alignment with visual cuts).

## Lab 2: Visual Semiotics & Framing Lab

Examines the visual composition, lighting, camera motion, and on-screen text:

1. **0.0s Opening Frame Audit**:
   - Focal Subject: Face, object, or text?
   - Spatial Environment: Kitchen, car, clinic, supermarket, nature.
   - Prop Semiotics: What is held in hand? (Stethoscope, mason jar, lemon, supplement bottle).
2. **Camera Mechanics**:
   - Distance: Extreme close-up (ECU), medium close-up (MCU), wide.
   - Movement: Static tripod, subtle handheld drift, aggressive snap zoom.
   - Pacing: Timestamp of every cut. Average shot duration.
3. **Typography & Overlay Architecture**:
   - Font style: Bold sans-serif, handwritten, terminal mono.
   - Color contrast: Yellow on black, white with black outline.
   - Placement: Safe zone compliance (avoiding Reels UI right-rail buttons and bottom caption).

## Lab 3: Script & Cognitive Friction Lab

Examines language architecture, narrative progression, and clarity:

1. **The Hook Sentence**:
   - Exact word count (target: 6-12 words).
   - Hook typology: Provocative question, shocking statement, myth debunk, warning.
2. **The Slippery Slide Progression**:
   - Beat 1 (0-3s): The Hook & Pattern Interrupt.
   - Beat 2 (3-8s): Problem Amplification & Shared Agony.
   - Beat 3 (8-18s): Mechanism Explanation & Root Cause.
   - Beat 4 (18-35s): The Protocol / The Remedy in Action.
   - Beat 5 (35-45s+): Frictionless CTA & Infinite Replay Loop.
3. **Cognitive Load Assessment**:
   - Reading grade level (Flesch-Kincaid target: Grade 5-7).
   - Elimination of academic jargon in favor of visceral analogies.

## Lab 4: Audience & Psychographic Diagnostic Lab

Mines the comment section to extract ground-truth audience reception:

1. **Validation & Identification Rate**:
   - Percentage of comments stating 'This is me', 'I feel seen', 'Happens every time'.
2. **Objection & Skepticism Mapping**:
   - Pinpoint what claims viewers push back against.
3. **Purchase & Implementation Intent**:
   - Comments asking: 'Where do I get this?', 'How much do I take?', 'Can kids take this?'.
4. **ManyChat & Automated Funnel Engagement**:
   - Exact trigger keyword detection (e.g. 'TEA', 'RECIPE', 'GUIDE').
   - Ratio of trigger keyword comments to total comments.

## Lab 5: Structural DNA & Format Lab

Classifies the video into proven viral chassis:

1. **Format Identification**:
   - E.g., The Supermarket Deception Audit, The Automotive Doctor Confession, The 3-Ingredient Kitchen Alchemy, The Taboo Symptom Exposé.
2. **Chassis Replicability**:
   - Can this format be executed by an AI avatar or actor in 20 minutes?
   - What fixed assets are required?
3. **Virality Multiplier Synthesis**:
   - Synthesizing why the video broke through algorithm thresholds (Retention + Shareability + Comment Density).
