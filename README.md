## 🎯 Overview & Mission

When analyzing viral reels, shorts, or VSLs, generic LLMs output superficial summaries like *"This video is engaging and uses good storytelling"*.

**Viral Content Deep Analyst** eliminates fluff by equipping your AI agent with **The 5 Lenses of Perception**:

1. **The Direct-Response Copywriter** (Sugarman & Whitman):
   Tracks word kinetic friction, the exact millisecond hook boundary, the Slippery Slide, and sensory nouns vs dead abstractions.
2. **The Clinical Persuasion Psychologist** (Cialdini):
   Decodes automatic compliance triggers (*"Click, Whirr"*), perceptual contrast, social proof validation, and pre-suasive attentional primes.
3. **The Audience Profiler** (Deterministic Behavioral Persona):
   Bans vague demographic labels (*"Women 25-45"*) and enforces a strict 5-field causal schema: `trigger`, `functional_job`, `emotional_job`, `solution_bias`, `root_cause`.
4. **The Visual Director & Semiotician**:
   Audits spatial psychology, environmental cues, prop authority (stethoscope vs mason jar vs supermarket cart), micro-expressions, color grading, and typography hierarchies.
5. **The Pattern Taxonomist**:
   Extracts structural DNA, modular beats, and calculates objective remixability scores for content factories.

## 📚 Synthesized Theoretical Foundations

### 1. Drew Eric Whitman's *CA$HVERTISING*
- **Life-Force 8 (LF8)**: The 8 biological survival drives hardwired into human DNA (Survival, Food/Drink, Pain Freedom, Sexual Companionship, Comfort, Superiority, Protecting Loved Ones, Social Approval).
- **17 Foundational Laws of Consumer Psychology**: Fear appeal calibration, Elaboration Likelihood Model (peripheral vs central processing), ego-involvement, and bandwagon contagion.
- **Direct-Response Secrets**: Mental movies, extreme specificity, tension-desire engine.

### 2. Joseph Sugarman's *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*
- **The Slippery Slide**: The sole purpose of the hook is to compel sentence 1; sentence 1 compels sentence 2; sentence 2 compels sentence 3.
- **The 15 Axioms of Direct Response**.
- **23 Video Copy Elements**: Hook banner, spoken premise, curiosity seed, pattern interrupt, mechanism, physical demonstration, objection preemption, loop anchor.
- **31 Psychological Triggers**: Involvement, honesty, integrity, proof of value, guilt relief, simplicity, specifics.

### 3. Robert Cialdini's *Influence* & *Pre-Suasion*
- **Fixed-Action Patterns (*"Click, Whirr"*)**: Trigger features that roll out automatic behavioral tapes.
- **The Contrast Principle**: Juxtaposing expensive/painful traditional procedures before revealing simple pantry alternatives.
- **The 7 Universal Weapons of Influence**: Reciprocity, Commitment & Consistency, Social Proof, Liking, Authority, Scarcity, Unity.
- **Privileged Moments & Single-Chute Framing**: Directing attention so the brain only searches for confirming evidence.
- **0-3s Visual Primes**: Spatial staging and semiotic props before words are spoken.

### 4. Deterministic 5-Field Behavioral Schema
Bans demographic hallucination. Demands 5 hard causal fields:
- `trigger`: The acute real-world catalyst or physical symptom prompting action.
- `functional_job`: The practical, tangible outcome the viewer needs.
- `emotional_job`: The internal relief from shame, anxiety, or fear.
- `solution_bias`: The viewer's pre-existing beliefs, skepticism, and past failed remedies.
- `root_cause`: The underlying biological, systemic, or psychological reality.

### 5. The 5 Forensic Multimodal Sensory Labs
- **Lab 1: Acoustic & Prosody Lab** (Words per minute, vocal inflection, audio layering).
- **Lab 2: Visual Semiotics & Framing Lab** (0.0s frame, cut density, camera movement, prop semiotics).
- **Lab 3: Script & Cognitive Friction Lab** (Flesch-Kincaid complexity, slippery slide beats).
- **Lab 4: Audience & Psychographic Diagnostic Lab** (Comment mining, ManyChat keyword tracking).
- **Lab 5: Structural DNA & Format Lab** (Format classification, remixability score).

## 🚦 Mandatory Anti-Slop Rules

Every output generated under this skill must adhere to these invariants:
- **No Em-Dashes**: Never use unicode em-dashes (`—` or `–`).
- **No Vague Demographics**: Never output `Target: Women 25-45`. Always output the 5-field schema.
- **Mandatory Confidence Scores**: Tag all persona assignments and format deductions with `[Confidence: High / Moderate / Speculative]`.
- **Sensory Primacy**: Native video/audio inspection always supersedes decoy or affiliate caption text.
