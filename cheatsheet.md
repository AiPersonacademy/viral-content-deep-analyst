# 📋 Viral Content Deep Analyst: Quick Cheat Sheet

## 1. The 5 Lenses Diagnostic Checklist

When auditing any video asset, confirm coverage across all 5 lenses:

- [ ] **Direct-Response Copywriter**:
  - Measured exact 0-3s hook boundary (seconds & word count).
  - Evaluated sentence-to-sentence reading gravity (The Slippery Slide).
  - Identified sensory nouns & active verbs vs abstract filler.
- [ ] **Clinical Persuasion Psychologist**:
  - Identified primary Life-Force 8 (LF8) drive.
  - Pinpointed "Click, Whirr" trigger stimulus and resulting behavior.
  - Audited Weapons of Influence (Reciprocity, Authority, Scarcity, etc.).
- [ ] **Audience Profiler**:
  - Populated all 5 behavioral fields: `trigger`, `functional_job`, `emotional_job`, `solution_bias`, `root_cause`.
  - Discarded broad demographic labels.
- [ ] **Visual Director & Semiotician**:
  - Inspected t = 0.0s opening frame (prop, subject, gaze, lighting).
  - Calculated cut density (seconds per cut).
  - Analyzed spatial geography (car, kitchen, supermarket aisle, office).
- [ ] **Pattern Taxonomist**:
  - Assigned format name and categorized as Proven or Novel.
  - Calculated Remixability Score (1-10) for content factory production.

## 3. Cialdini's Weapons of Influence Matrix

| Weapon | Video Execution | Audience Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reciprocity** | Free high-value recipe / dosage guide | High compulsion to comment keyword |
| **Commitment** | 'Type YES if you agree' / 'Save this' | Identity lock-in to the protocol |
| **Social Proof** | 'Look at the 3,000 comments from last week' | Bandwagon validation ('I am not alone') |
| **Liking** | Unvarnished intimacy in car / kitchen | Trust and parasocial affinity |
| **Authority** | Scrub badge, stethoscope, anatomical model | Immediate suspension of skepticism |
| **Scarcity** | 'Only available wild-harvested' / 'Video will be deleted' | Urgent FOMO and save rate |
| **Unity** | 'Mothers who reject corporate processed foods' | Fierce tribal alignment against villain |

## 4. Confidence Labeling Rules

| Tag | Standard of Proof |
| :--- | :--- |
| `[Confidence: High]` | Backed by explicit spoken words, direct visual proof, or verified comment patterns. |
| `[Confidence: Moderate]` | Strongly supported by structural alignment and subtext. |
| `[Confidence: Speculative]` | Plausible working hypothesis requiring further sample validation. |
